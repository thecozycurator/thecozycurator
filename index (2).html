Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the cozy curator ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our application and its integration with Pinterest.

1. Purpose of the Application

the cozy curator is a personal-use application designed to help automate the creation, management, scheduling, and publishing of original lifestyle content on Pinterest.

The content may include glow-up tips, study motivation, self-care, productivity, beauty, organization, and related lifestyle content.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize the application to connect to your Pinterest account, the application may access information necessary to perform the requested Pinterest API functions, such as:

  • Your Pinterest account information necessary for authentication.
  • Your Pinterest Boards and Board information.
  • Pins created or managed through the application.
  • Information required to publish content to your authorized Pinterest account.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to:

  • Authenticate and connect your Pinterest account.
  • Create and publish Pins.
  • Manage Boards when necessary for publishing.
  • Schedule or manage content.
  • Maintain records of content published through the application.
  • Troubleshoot and maintain the application.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your Pinterest account information with third parties except when necessary to operate the application or comply with applicable laws.

5. Data Storage

We aim to collect and retain only the information necessary to operate the application. Authentication credentials, access tokens, and other sensitive information are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

6. Pinterest

The application interacts with Pinterest through Pinterest's official API. Your use of Pinterest remains subject to Pinterest's own terms and policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality.

You may request deletion of information associated with the application by contacting us through the contact information provided below. You may also revoke the application's access to your Pinterest account through the appropriate Pinterest account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
